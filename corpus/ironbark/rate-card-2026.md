# Rate card 2026

_Ironbark Line Freight · effective 1 July 2026 · placeholder document for slice 1_

## Metro Melbourne

General freight within metro Melbourne is charged at $96 per pallet, with a minimum charge of $180
per consignment. Tail-lift delivery adds $45 per drop.

## Regional Victoria

Regional general freight is charged at $1.85 per kilometre per pallet space, with a minimum charge of
$420. Delivery to Mildura, Horsham and Warrnambool is on a fixed weekly schedule.

## Fuel levy

A fuel levy of 14.5% applies to all line-haul charges. It is reviewed monthly against the national
average diesel price and published on the first business day of each month.
