# Driver fatigue policy

_Ironbark Line Freight · version 3 · placeholder document for slice 1_

## Work and rest limits

Drivers work under Standard Hours from the Heavy Vehicle National Law. In any 24-hour period a driver
must not work more than 12 hours and must have at least 7 continuous hours of stationary rest.

## Short breaks

Drivers take at least 15 minutes of rest after every 5 hours 15 minutes of work. Breaks are recorded
in the electronic work diary, not on paper.

## Reporting fatigue

Any driver who feels fatigued must stop as soon as it is safe and call the depot on the fatigue line.
No driver is penalised for stopping because of fatigue. This is part of our Chain of Responsibility
duties.
