# Standard service level agreement

_Ironbark Line Freight · placeholder document for slice 1_

## On-time delivery

Ironbark commits to delivering 97% of metro consignments on the booked day. On-time performance is
reported to each customer monthly.

## Damage claims

Customers must report damaged freight within 48 hours of delivery, with photos. Claims are assessed
within 10 business days.

## Escalation

Service issues go first to the customer's account manager. Unresolved issues are escalated to the
Operations Manager at the Laverton depot within one business day.
